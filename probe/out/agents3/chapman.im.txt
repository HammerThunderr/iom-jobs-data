Our Services
Agency
We are estate agents and property consultants handling the sale, purchase and leasing of commercial & residential property in the Isle of Man. Our commercial team also advise on capital values, yields, rents and current market conditions.
Investment
We advise property companies, financial institutions, pension funds, corporate clients and private clients on the sale, purchase and funding of commercial & residential investment property across all sectors.
Valuation
We provide independent RICS Red Book appraisals of commercial property for secured lending, probate, company accounts and stock exchange listings.
Asset management
Managing commercial property is a core activity at Chapman Chartered Surveyors. We manage commercial & residential property in the Isle of Man and UK for clients including pension funds, property companies, developers, trusts and private investors.
Lease advisory
Restructuring a commercial lease can provide opportunity to enhance the property’s capital value or reduce the tenant’s operating costs. We provide a comprehensive range of services
including submissions to arbitrators and independent experts.
Rent review
Negotiation of rent reviews and lease renewals is a skilled area that requires knowledge of case law and statutory provisions, and a good understanding of the market. When rent reviews cannot be agreed, we can present a detailed submission to a third party determination surveyor.
Development
Our core services include development appraisals, development management, funding advice, disposals and acquisitions, site finding and planning advice.
Residential Lettings
We believe that buy-to-let companies and private landlords should receive the same level of service and expert advice as corporate or commercial investors. All instructions are managed by RICS qualified surveyors.
Experienced
All of our professional staff are Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors (RICS) qualified.  All instructions are managed by a Director, ensuring the highest possible levels of care at all times.
Professional
The lasting relationships we have formed with leading businesses and investors are testament to the service we provide.  Over 90% of our work is generated from existing or previous clients.
Established
We have provided expert advice on property matters since the company was founded in 1973.
