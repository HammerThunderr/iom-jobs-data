Why sell your home with BGC?

BGC has been part of the Isle of Man’s property story since 1994 – more than thirty years of local sales data, relationships and hard-won judgement. You get a fully qualified residential team from market appraisal through to key handover, professional photography marketing material, and a high-tech platform that keeps you informed at every stage. Regulated, qualified, accountable. We would not have it any other way.

How do I book a property viewing with BGC?

Find the property on bgc.im and use the “Book a viewing” button, or call your nearest BGC branch. Choose a date and time that suits you and you will get a reminder before the appointment.

How do I make an offer on a property?

The moment your viewing ends, we will ask for your feedback by text, WhatsApp or email – and you can submit your offer in the same reply. You can also make an offer in person at any BGC branch, over the phone or in writing. Whichever route you choose, a BGC property professional will guide you through what happens next.

How long does it take to buy a house in the Isle of Man?

At BGC, purchases currently take an average of 10-14 weeks from accepted offer to completion. The exact timeline depends on the length of the chain, how quickly your mortgage is approved and the vendor’s own circumstances. Cash purchases with no chain can complete considerably faster.

Online estate agent or high street agent – what is the difference?

Most online estate agents don’t have a high street presence or prominent office location. A ‘local property expert’, who can sometimes be responsible for a large area, will visit your home to give you a suggested asking price. Once your property is listed in most cases you’ll deal with a separate dedicated member of staff for viewings, likewise for sales progression too. In contrast, at BGC, we offer the best of both worlds – Our slick, bold and contemporary branding showcases our interactive website offering all the bells whistles of an online agency, yet, our prominent high street presence also enables a more personal approach and our team are in place ready to welcome those who walk through our door. The BGC team are a highly trained, experienced group of property professionals who work closely together, ensuring that we are all with you throughout, from appraisal to completion – our entire residential team are with you every step of the way. Another difference is that most online estate agents take a form of upfront payment, this can dilute the incentive to sell your property.

We offer a no sale, no charge option – our value is our success.
