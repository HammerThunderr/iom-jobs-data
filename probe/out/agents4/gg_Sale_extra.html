Summary

The Old Smithy offers a truly unique and versatile lifestyle opportunity, rarely available in today’s market. Nestled in an idyllic position on the edge of the village, the property enjoys a peaceful setting with delightful countryside views to the front, while remaining within easy reach of local amenities including a shop and primary school. The vibrant town of Ramsey is just a short drive away, offering a wider range of shops, restaurants, and services.

The main residence boasts charming and characterful accommodation, perfect for those seeking a home with warmth, personality, and flexibility. A welcoming entrance hallway with cloakroom (WC) leads to two well-proportioned reception areas, including a cosy living room and an open dining room flowing through to a country-style kitchen, with a utility room located off the kitchen for added practicality. Upstairs, the property offers 2 to 3 bedrooms and a family bathroom—please note that the third bedroom has limited head height and may be better suited as a home office or nursery.

Adjoining the main house is The Blacksmith’s Cottage, a 4-star Gold rated holiday let . This beautifully converted annex is ideal for generating supplementary income as a holiday let, or as accommodation for a multi-generational family (subject to any necessary approvals). The cottage features an impressive open-plan living, dining, and kitchen area with stunning exposed beams and a log burner, creating a cosy and welcoming atmosphere. There is also a separate double bedroom and an ensuite shower room, all finished to a high standard.

Externally, the property continues to impress with a large gated driveway to the rear offering excellent privacy and ample parking, along with a detached double garage/workshop—perfect for hobbyists or those in need of extra storage. A south-facing paved patio provides a fantastic space for entertaining or relaxing in the sun.

Offering a blend of character, comfort, and income potential, The Old Smithy and Blacksmith’s Cottage represent a rare opportunity in a truly picturesque setting. Early viewing is highly recommended to fully appreciate all that this exceptional home has to offer.
