    An exceptional turnkey residence set in approximately two acres, with interiors by Roisin Quinn Design Studio, a detached two bedroom cottage, three car garage with room above, landscaped gardens and elevated rural and coastal views.

    Primrose Lodge is an exceptional newly built detached residence occupying an elevated position within a sought after rural and coastal setting. Set within grounds extending to approximately two acres, the property combines a substantial principal home with a detached two bedroom cottage, triple garage and flexible ancillary accommodation.

    The interiors were professionally conceived by Roisin Quinn Design Studio, founded by Roisin Quinn, winner of BBC’s Interior Design Masters. Every room has been carefully considered, bringing together bespoke design, established design houses, individual craftsmanship and a confident use of colour, pattern and texture.

    The impressive reception hall immediately establishes the character of the property, with a dramatic central staircase, bespoke ironwork, decorative flooring and carefully selected lighting. Little Greene paints are used throughout, complemented by wallcoverings from Adam Ellis, Lick, House of Hackney, Harlequin and Sanderson.

    The principal living spaces continue the distinctive design approach. Bespoke joinery provides both visual impact and practical storage, while the living room incorporates a bespoke fitted bar. Statement lighting includes pieces from Pure White Lines and Rothschild & Bickers, together with chandeliers and individually designed pendants.

    The kitchen has been completed to a particularly high specification, with a substantial central island, bespoke island lighting, a Quooker boiling water tap, Villeroy & Boch sinks, Perrin & Rowe taps and bespoke joinery by Terry Ditchfield. A bespoke kitchen sideboard and ovens are included within the sale.

    The bathrooms are equally impressive, combining Original Style and Bisazza tiles with Thomas Crapper sanitaryware and Burlington fittings. Bold patterns, elegant brassware, statement chandeliers and individually selected finishes create rooms that are both practical and visually memorable.

    Detached Cottage

    A substantial detached cottage provides excellent independent accommodation for guests, extended family or those seeking additional private living space. The cottage offers two bedrooms, each with its own ensuite facilities. The first floor comprises an open plan kitchen, dining and living area opening onto a private terrace, positioned to enjoy the surrounding rural landscape and elevated coastal views.

    A detached three car garage provides excellent parking, workshop and storage space.

    Above the garage is a substantial room offering considerable flexibility. It would be ideally suited as a gym, home office, cinema room, studio or games room.

    To the rear is a sheltered and private sun terrace with a hot tub and dedicated dining area. A stairway leads from the terrace into the main garden, which is predominantly laid to lawn and complemented by mature shrubs, established trees and planting.

    A storage container within the grounds, currently used to house garden machinery, is included within the sale.

    The combination of a substantial principal residence, independent cottage, triple garage, flexible accommodation, extensive grounds and elevated views makes Primrose Lodge a particularly distinctive lifestyle property.

    Inclusions - Fixtures and fittings. Specifically included within the sale are the chandeliers in the landing, principal bedroom and ensuite, bespoke island pendants, kitchen sideboard, living room bar, ovens and storage container.

    The remaining furniture, furnishings, artwork and decorative pieces are available by separate negotiation. This includes selected pieces by Duresta, Natuzzi, Jonathan Adler, Wendy Morrison Design and Oliver Bonas.

    Appliances - All integrated appliances

    Tenure, Freehold

    Heating - Oil. Individual boilers serve the main house and cottage.
